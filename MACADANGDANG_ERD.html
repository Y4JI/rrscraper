DIAGRAM LEGEND & CARDINALITY NOTATION PostgreSQL 17 | Supabase Hosted | Django 5.2
Field Keys:
PK Primary Key
FK Foreign Key
UQ Unique Field
Crow's Foot Notation:
—||— Mandatory One (1)
—< Mandatory Many (N)
—o< Zero or Many (0..N)
—o|— Zero or One (0..1)
Primary Relationships:
• auth_user (1) ↔ (1) profile_profile / user_settings
• auth_user (1) ↔ (N) scraper_job / tracker_bookmark
• scraper_novel (1) ↔ (N) scraper_chapter
Domain Logic:
• scraper_novel (1) ↔ (0..N) scraper_job
• scraper_novel (M) ↔ (N) scraper_tag (via novel_tags)
• tracker_bookmark (N) ↔ (0..1) scraper_chapter
